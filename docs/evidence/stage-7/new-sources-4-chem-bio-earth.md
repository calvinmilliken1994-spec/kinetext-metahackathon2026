## Page styles our components may not serve yet

For the lead's subject-engine design (from this slice's 16 pages):

1. **Wide numeric data tables** — `nasa-nssdc-planetary-fact-sheet-metric` (21 properties × 10 bodies) and `medplus-inheritance-patterns` (8 patterns × 3 text columns). The compare-table component takes entity × attribute rows, but a 10-body × 21-property grid needs row/column slicing guidance (e.g. pick 3–4 bodies × 4–6 properties) or it will overflow; a 'read a value out of a table' quiz type (cell lookup by row+column) is not served by any current component.
2. **Time-series records as image charts** — `noaa-climate-gov-atmospheric-carbon-dioxide` (annual CO2/ppm and growth-rate history) and climate.gov's temperature sibling: the teachable numbers live in prose and captions while the year-by-year series are images. No component plots or quizzes a time series; a future engine could pair such pages with the real chart images (see 4).
3. **Chemistry notation** — inline ionic equations with superscript charges (`H2O = H^+ + OH^-` in `usgs-wss-ph-and-water`), subscripts (`CO2`, `H2O`), a base-10 log energy relation (`log E = 5.24 + 1.44Mw` in `usgs-earthquake-magnitude-energy-intensity`), and staged-cycle equations with subscripts (`T4=T3+Q/cv`, `p4=p3+T4/T3` in `nasa-grc-combustion-process`). The formula explorer's maths language and the checkers need subscript/superscript-aware token matching (the species-boundary rule from the kit-checking notes applies) and log support; none of the current engines parses a chemical equation.
4. **Labelled diagrams carrying key structure** — MedlinePlus central-dogma figure (protein production), cell-organelle diagrams, DNA double-helix figure; USGS pH-scale diagram and meter photograph; NASA's labelled Wright-1903 engine drawing; JetStream's atmosphere temperature-profile and Köppen-zone maps. Stored as caption lines only (images not stored, per project rules on generated illustrations). A future diagram engine could use the REAL page images (all are public-domain or agency-credited); captions are kept verbatim to make that possible.
5. **Tabbed pages** — `usgs-wss-ph-and-water` serves Overview/Science/Multimedia panels; the transcription flattens them linearly, including the page's own duplicated callout variants. A tab-aware engine could present panels separately; quote-grounding must tolerate the near-duplicates.
6. **Ordered numbered lists with historical attributions** — `noaa-jetstream-global-atmospheric-circulations` (1. Hadley 1735, 2. Ferrel 1856, 3. Polar): a sequence/timeline hybrid where steps carry dates and people; the sequence component has no attribution slot.
7. **Mendelian ratios / Punnett squares** — NOT found in-range on any licence-verified page: `medplus-inheritance-patterns` gives categories and mechanisms but no ratio arithmetic, and no stored page contains a Punnett-square grid. A ratios engine still lacks a licensed source page (genome.gov glossary entries with ratios are below the 1,500-char floor; OpenStax is licence-blocked).
8. **Multi-step stoichiometry worked examples** — likewise not found in-range (NASA's gas pages give single-step relations; the worked-example-rich textbooks are licence-blocked). The nearest served style is the USGS magnitude page's chained hypothetical-rupture arithmetic (fault length + slip → Mw), which is multi-value but not multi-equation.

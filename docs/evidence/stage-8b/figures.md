# Stage 8b Part 1 — figure library review (`data/figures/`)

Date: 2026-10-31 (retrievals 02:42–03:12 UTC). Author: research subagent (Qwen 3.8 Max route), reviewed against the frozen format in `docs/specs/STAGE-8b-label-diagram.md` Part 1.
Result: **9 figures** written to `data/figures/` (image bytes + one JSON each), within the 8–12 target. All 9 pass the format, size, licence, active-source and "page text names ≥ 3 parts" checks (recomputed from disk, see *Verification*).

## Method
1. Candidate pages: every **active** library page in `data/sources/` whose subject is biology, earth-science, chemistry or physics (retired pages skipped). Each page's HTML was fetched with Node `fetch` (Python HTTPS fails on this host) and every `<figure>`/`<img>` was listed with its `alt`, caption and any credit line; 35 candidate images were downloaded.
2. Bytes were taken exactly as served (no re-encode, no resize). `sha256` and `width`/`height` were computed in Node from the downloaded bytes (SHA-256 over the buffer; dimensions parsed from the PNG `IHDR`, JPEG `SOFn` and GIF logical-screen-descriptor headers — no image libraries installed).
3. `caption`, `altText` and `credit` were extracted programmatically from the stored page HTML (MedlinePlus `<div class="caption">` / `<div class="image-credit">`, NOAA and NASA `<figcaption>` with the "Download Image" utility link removed), with HTML entities decoded to the characters the page renders. They are not retyped.
4. **Printed labels** (`labelsInImage`) and the diagram-vs-photo-vs-chart call were made by looking at each downloaded image and listing its printed text verbatim; the lists are quoted per figure below. Figures marked "not inspected" in the rejected list were judged only from the page's own `alt`/caption text and are labelled as such.
5. **≥ 3 parts named on the page**: each figure's part names were matched as whole tokens against `data/sources/<sourceId>.txt` (the library page's own text); the matching sentences are quoted per figure.
6. Licence: the publisher's policy page was re-fetched on 2026-10-31 and the operative sentences quoted into each JSON's `licenseEvidence`; the per-image credit printed on the page (or inside the image) was checked against the spec's third-party rule.

Working files (page HTML, download scripts, contact sheets, the 35-image candidate set) live in the local scratch folder (not committed).

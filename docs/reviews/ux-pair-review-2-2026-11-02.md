## What works (both)
- Honesty is consistent everywhere: the replay is labelled "Replay of a recorded run · 4× · recorded …"; the provenance line, "Teacher review pending", the trust receipt, the "Live generation off · stored runs only" title bar, and the Results method notes (pre-registered, timeouts disclosed, the diagram zero explained). (Terra + Opus)
- The Demo's repair story is the strongest Meta moment in the app: SAM outlines, a check fails ("each number sits on its part…"), Muse Spark revises as revision 2, and it passes. The cards are well placed and step 2 says plainly that the recording costs nothing. (Opus; Terra saw steps 1–2)
- The sidebar is calm and matches dev.meta.ai rather than a generic dashboard. Current-view highlight, the Source page toggle and the access footer ("Read-only demo · Enter access code") are all clear. The New lesson split (library | your own source) reads at a glance. (Both)
- The access form never leaves a dead Build button. In the locked state Build becomes the inline judge form with the spec wording. (Opus, through the shim)
- Keyboard: a visible 2 px blue ring on sidebar items, path tabs, chips, library rows (`:has(input:focus-visible)`), summaries and theme toggle. Arrow keys move through the sidebar, the radio list is one tab stop, All lessons rows are buttons, and focus moves to the view title on navigation. No focus defect found. (Opus)
- Presentation: R reveals, → advances, nothing clips at 1920, 2560 or 1366. The diagram tap-to-place, Check and Hint work. Sequence move buttons are labelled. (Terra)
- Results: headline 97% / 92% with Wilson intervals, the side-by-side table, per-part-type bars, and every number linked to its committed file. (Both)
- 1366×768 still fits (sidebar 212 px, no document overflow). The light theme has no dark leftovers. (Opus)
